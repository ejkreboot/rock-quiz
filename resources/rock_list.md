# 🧪 **Minerals**

1. **Augite (pyroxene)** – Dark green to black; blocky, stubby crystals; 2 cleavages at ~90°.
   *Composition:* Ca-Fe-Mg silicate.
   *Formation:* Crystallizes in mafic/intermediate igneous rocks.
   *Occurs in:* **Basalt**, **Gabbro**, **Andesite**.

2. **Biotite (mica)** – Black/brown; splits into flexible sheets; glassy luster.
   *Composition:* K-Fe-Mg mica.
   *Formation:* Intermediate–felsic magmas or metamorphism.
   *Occurs in:* **Granite**, **Schist**, **Gneiss**.

3. **Calcite** – White or colorless; reacts vigorously with acid; rhombohedral cleavage (3 not at 90°).
   *Composition:* CaCO₃.
   *Formation:* Precipitation or biological secretion in marine settings.
   *Occurs in:* **Limestone**, metamorphs to **Marble**.

4. **Chalcopyrite** – Brass-yellow metallic; tarnishes iridescent purple; softer than pyrite.
   *Composition:* CuFeS₂.
   *Formation:* Hydrothermal veins.
   *Associated with:* **Pyrite**, **Galena**.

5. **Fluorite** – Purple, green, or clear; 4 perfect cleavages (octahedral); glassy.
   *Composition:* CaF₂.
   *Formation:* Hydrothermal deposits or sedimentary veins.
   *Associated with:* **Galena**, **Calcite**.

6. **Galena** – Metallic silver-gray; cubic cleavage (3 at 90°); very dense.
   *Composition:* PbS.
   *Formation:* Hydrothermal veins in limestones.
   *Primary ore of lead.*

7. **Garnet** – Commonly red/brown; glassy dodecahedral crystals; no cleavage.
   *Composition:* Fe-Mg-Ca-Al silicate.
   *Formation:* Medium–high grade metamorphism.
   *Occurs in:* **Schist**, **Gneiss**.

8. **Goethite** – Brown to black; earthy to submetallic; brownish streak.
   *Composition:* FeO(OH).
   *Formation:* Weathering product of Fe minerals.
   *Occurs in:* Soils, bog iron, oxidized **Hematite** zones.

9. **Graphite** – Silvery gray; greasy feel; very soft; marks paper.
   *Composition:* Carbon.
   *Formation:* Metamorphism of organic matter.
   *Occurs in:* **Schist**, **Marble**.

10. **Gypsum** – Colorless to white; very soft (scratched by fingernail); one perfect cleavage.
    *Composition:* CaSO₄·2H₂O.
    *Formation:* Evaporite deposits.
    *Occurs in:* **Rock gypsum**, precursor to **Anhydrite**.

11. **Halite** – Colorless; cubic cleavage; salty taste.
    *Composition:* NaCl.
    *Formation:* Evaporite mineral in arid basins.
    *Occurs in:* **Rock salt**.

12. **Hematite** – Metallic to earthy red/brown; reddish streak; heavy.
    *Composition:* Fe₂O₃.
    *Formation:* Oxidation of iron minerals or chemical precipitation.
    *Occurs in:* Banded iron formations.

13. **Hornblende (amphibole)** – Black; splintery habit; 2 cleavages at 60° and 120°.
    *Composition:* Ca-Na-Mg-Fe amphibole.
    *Formation:* Intermediate–felsic magmas and metamorphism.
    *Occurs in:* **Andesite**, **Diorite**, **Amphibolite**.

14. **Kaolinite** – White, dull, powdery; earthy odor when damp; very soft.
    *Composition:* Al₂Si₂O₅(OH)₄.
    *Formation:* Weathering of feldspar in humid climates.
    *Occurs in:* **Shale**, clay deposits.

15. **Magnetite** – Black; magnetic; metallic to submetallic.
    *Composition:* Fe₃O₄.
    *Formation:* Igneous and metamorphic origin.
    *Occurs in:* **Gabbro**, **Basalt**, **BIFs**.

16. **Muscovite (mica)** – Colorless to silvery; splits into elastic sheets.
    *Composition:* K-Al silicate mica.
    *Formation:* Felsic igneous and metamorphic rocks.
    *Occurs in:* **Granite**, **Schist**, **Pegmatite**.

17. **Olivine** – Olive-green; granular (sugary texture); no cleavage.
    *Composition:* (Mg,Fe)₂SiO₄.
    *Formation:* Early crystallization from mafic magma.
    *Occurs in:* **Basalt**, **Peridotite**; alters to **Serpentine**.

18. **Plagioclase feldspar** – White to gray; striations on cleavage faces; 2 cleavages ~90°.
    *Composition:* Na-Ca feldspar series.
    *Occurs in:* Most igneous rocks (esp. **Basalt**, **Andesite**, **Gabbro**, **Diorite**).

19. **Potassium feldspar** – Pink to white; often with exsolution lamellae; 2 cleavages ~90°.
    *Composition:* K-Al-Si feldspar.
    *Occurs in:* **Granite**, **Rhyolite**.

20. **Pyrite** – Brassy yellow metallic; cubic crystals; greenish-black streak.
    *Composition:* FeS₂.
    *Formation:* Hydrothermal and sedimentary.
    *Alters to:* **Goethite**, **Hematite**.

21. **Quartz** – Colorless to many colors; glassy; conchoidal fracture; no cleavage.
    *Composition:* SiO₂.
    *Very stable.*
    *Occurs in:* **Granite**, **Sandstone**, **Quartzite**.

22. **Sphalerite** – Brown to black; resinous luster; sulfur odor when scratched; yellow streak.
    *Composition:* ZnS.
    *Formation:* Hydrothermal veins.
    *Main ore of zinc.*

23. **Sulfur** – Bright yellow; smells when rubbed; soft.
    *Composition:* Native element.
    *Formation:* Volcanic and evaporite settings.

24. **Talc** – White to green; pearly; very soft (soapy feel).
    *Composition:* Mg silicate.
    *Formation:* Hydrothermal alteration of mafic rocks.
    *Occurs in:* **Serpentinite**, **Soapstone**.

# 🌋 **Igneous Rocks**

1. **Andesite** – Gray; fine-grained; intermediate composition; often porphyritic.
   *Minerals:* Plagioclase, amphibole, pyroxene.
   *Environment:* Volcanic arcs (subduction zones).
   *Intrusive equivalent:* **Diorite**.

2. **Basalt** – Black; fine-grained; mafic; sometimes vesicular.
   *Minerals:* Pyroxene, Ca-plagioclase, olivine.
   *Environment:* Oceanic crust, lava flows.
   *Intrusive equivalent:* **Gabbro**.

3. **Diorite** – “Salt and pepper” coarse-grained mix of white and black minerals.
   *Minerals:* Plagioclase + hornblende.
   *Intrusive equivalent of:* **Andesite**.

4. **Gabbro** – Coarse-grained dark rock; mostly pyroxene + plagioclase.
   *Intrusive equivalent of:* **Basalt**.

5. **Granite** – Coarse-grained; pink or gray; quartz + feldspar + mica.
   *Felsic, continental crust.*
   *Extrusive equivalent:* **Rhyolite**.

6. **Obsidian** – Glassy black volcanic glass; conchoidal fracture; sharp edges.
   *Same composition as rhyolite but cooled too fast for crystals.*

7. **Peridotite** – Coarse-grained; greenish (olivine-rich); ultramafic.
   *Mantle rock; parent to basaltic magma.*
   *Alters to:* **Serpentinite**.

8. **Pumice** – Light gray; frothy; very light and floats; felsic.
   *Vesicular volcanic glass from explosive eruptions.*

9. **Rhyolite** – Light-colored; fine-grained; felsic; often with quartz phenocrysts.
   *Extrusive equivalent of:* **Granite**.

10. **Scoria** – Dark red/black; vesicular; heavier than pumice; mafic.
    *Basaltic lava with gas bubbles.*

11. **Volcanic breccia** – Angular volcanic fragments cemented together.
    *Formed by explosive eruptions or volcanic landslides.*

# 🪨 **Sedimentary Rocks**

1. **Arkose sandstone** – Coarse-grained; pinkish; rich in feldspar.
   *Environment:* Alluvial fans near granitic source.
   *Matures into:* **Quartz sandstone**.

2. **Breccia** – Angular rock fragments in a finer matrix.
   *Forms near source areas (faults, talus slopes).*
   *Related to:* **Conglomerate** (rounded clasts).

3. **Chert** – Hard, dense; conchoidal fracture; dull to waxy luster.
   *Composition:* Microcrystalline quartz.
   *Environment:* Deep marine; chemical or biogenic silica.
   *Metamorphic equivalent:* **Quartzite**.

4. **Coal** – Black; lightweight; sooty streak; organic origin.
   *Forms from compaction of plant material in swamps.*
   *Metamorphic equivalent:* **Graphite**.

5. **Conglomerate** – Rounded pebbles cemented together.
   *Environment:* Rivers or beaches with strong currents.*

6. **Dolostone** – Reacts weakly with acid when powdered; tan-gray color.
   *Composition:* CaMg(CO₃)₂.
   *Formation:* Mg-rich fluids altering **Limestone**.
   *Metamorphic equivalent:* **Dolomitic marble**.

7. **Limestone** – Reacts with acid; usually gray to tan.
   *Composition:* Calcite.
   *Environment:* Marine; biological or chemical origin.
   *Metamorphic equivalent:* **Marble**.

   *Subtypes:*

   * **Chalk** – Soft, white, powdery; microfossil origin.
   * **Coquina** – Visible shell fragments, loosely cemented.
   * **Fossiliferous** – Contains visible fossils.
   * **Micritic** – Very fine-grained, smooth.
   * **Oolitic** – Composed of small spherical grains (ooids).

8. **Quartz sandstone** – Gritty; mostly quartz; often light-colored.
   *Environment:* Beach or desert.
   *Metamorphic equivalent:* **Quartzite**.

9. **Rock gypsum** – Soft; white/pink; scratched by fingernail.
   *Evaporite deposit; CaSO₄·2H₂O.*

10. **Rock salt** – Transparent cubic crystals; salty taste.
    *Evaporite deposit; NaCl.*

11. **Shale** – Fine-grained; splits into thin sheets; dull luster.
    *Composition:* Clay minerals.
    *Environment:* Quiet water (lakes, deep marine).
    *Metamorphic equivalents:* **Slate → Phyllite → Schist → Gneiss**.

# 🔥 **Metamorphic Rocks**

1. **Amphibolite** – Dark, coarse-grained; amphibole-rich; may show weak foliation.
   *Parent:* **Basalt** or **Gabbro**.
   *Grade:* Medium–high.

2. **Gneiss** – Alternating light/dark bands; coarse foliation.
   *Parent:* **Granite** or **Schist**.
   *Grade:* High.

3. **Metaconglomerate** – Pebbles deformed or stretched within finer matrix.
   *Parent:* **Conglomerate**.
   *Grade:* Variable.

4. **Marble** – Crystalline; reacts with acid; sugary texture; usually white.
   *Parent:* **Limestone** or **Dolostone**.

5. **Phyllite** – Silky sheen; fine-grained; wavy foliation.
   *Parent:* **Slate** (from shale).
   *Grade:* Low–medium.

6. **Quartzite** – Hard; sugary texture; scratches glass; fused quartz grains.
   *Parent:* **Quartz sandstone**.
   *Grade:* Any.

7. **Schist** – Sparkly mica-rich foliation; medium to coarse-grained.
   *Parent:* **Phyllite** or **Shale**.
   *Grade:* Medium–high.

8. **Serpentinite** – Greenish, waxy luster; slick feel; derived from ultramafic rock.
   *Parent:* **Peridotite** (hydrated olivine).
   *Grade:* Low.

9. **Slate** – Dark gray; very fine-grained; splits into flat sheets with a “ping” sound.
   *Parent:* **Shale**.
   *Grade:* Low.
